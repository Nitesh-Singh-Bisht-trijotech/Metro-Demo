# Delhi Metro Station Facilities - Mock Knowledge Base for RAG

> **Data provenance note:** This is mock data in the same style as `dmrc_mock_data.md`. Station names, line membership and interchanges were compiled from public secondary sources (Wikipedia-style station lists, transit guides) and general knowledge, and may contain gaps or errors, especially for the Pink Line, Magenta Line and the March 2026 Phase 4 stations. **Per-station facility details are NOT published item-by-item on the official site in a form that could be read.** Every station therefore gets the same *typical baseline* of facilities (consistent with dmrc-025), plus only the few station-specific facts that a secondary source actually states (ATMs, parking, bus routes, landmarks). Layout (elevated or underground) is a best-effort tag and is marked `verify` where unsure. Do not use for real passenger decisions. Confirm in the DMRC Momentum Delhi Sarthi 2.0 app or at delhimetrorail.com.
> **Reference date:** October 2026.
> **Scope:** Delhi Metro lines operated by DMRC, plus Rapid Metro Gurugram (operated by DMRC since 2019). Noida Metro (Aqua Line) is a separate operator and is not covered.

---

## [F000] Typical facilities at a Delhi Metro station
- **id:** dmrc-fac-000
- **category:** facilities
- **tags:** baseline, accessibility, security, AFC
- **caution:** Generic baseline, not a per-station guarantee. Lift, escalator and toilet availability can differ by station and can be out of service.

security check and bag screening at the entrance; AFC flap gates for smart cards, NCMC and QR tickets; ticket counter and token/QR ticket vending; lifts and escalators; tactile paving and an accessible toilet for Divyang passengers; drinking water and toilets; CCTV and help points; first coach (direction of travel) marked for women on the platform; customer care desk; helpline 155370.
